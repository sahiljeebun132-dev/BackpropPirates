Honest about the last step. Android refuses ACTION_CALL for emergency numbers from every third-party app and CALL_PRIVILEGED is closed to us, so no app on the Play Store can dial an ambulance by itself. Apple’s own Crash Detection, with the hardware and the OS privileges, still counts down before calling. The texts and the call to a saved contact are genuinely automatic; 114 is one tap.
